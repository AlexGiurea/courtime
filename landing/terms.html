Terms

The deal, in plain language.

Last updated 30 July 2026

Courtime is run by Alex Giurea. Using it means agreeing to what's below. It is written to be read once, in full, in about two minutes.

What you get

A shared schedule for your club: the day grid, the clinic sheets, photo import with human review, printable day sheets, and every coach on their phone. That is the free tier and it stays free — not a trial. Pro adds the reporting and the Tempo assistant for $19 per club each month, flat, however many coaches you have. We'll give you notice before any price change and you can leave instead.

Your data is yours

Everything you put into Courtime belongs to your club. We don't sell it, we don't train anything on it, and you can ask for all of it back or have it deleted at any time — see Privacy. Cancel whenever you like; nothing is locked in and there is no contract to get out of.

What we ask of you

  • Only put people in the book that your club has a reason to hold — it is your club's record, and you are responsible for it.
  • Don't share staff logins outside the club, and tell us if one gets out.
  • Don't attack, overload or reverse-engineer the service.

The honest part about reliability

Courtime is early software run by one person. It will occasionally be unavailable, and the photo import — which is a machine reading handwriting — will sometimes get a booking wrong, which is exactly why nothing it reads is published until a person confirms it.

So: keep your paper book while you're trying this. That's the whole pilot design, and it's the honest answer to "what if it goes down." The service is provided as it is, without warranties, and our liability is limited to what you've paid us in the previous twelve months — which for a club on the free tier is nothing, and for a club on Pro is at most $228.

Ending it

Stop whenever you want, from Settings or by asking. We may close an account that is being used to break these terms or the law, and we'll say why. If we ever shut the service down, we'll give at least 30 days' notice and a way to take your data with you.
