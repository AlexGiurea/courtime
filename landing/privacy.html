Privacy

What Courtime stores, and why.

Last updated 30 July 2026

Courtime is a schedule. Almost everything in it was already written in a club's paper book — we are holding the same information, in a form that can be read on a phone. This page says exactly what that is in plain language, because a club handing over its members' names deserves better than eight pages of hedging.

What we hold

  • The schedule itself. Courts, times, what each booking is called, which coach is on it, and the notes column — as written by the club.
  • Clients, as the book names them. Names on bookings and clinic sign-up sheets, plus the phone numbers and NTRP ratings a club wrote beside them. These people are not our users and we never contact them.
  • Staff accounts. An email address, a name, a role, and a password we never see — it is stored hashed by our authentication provider.
  • Photographs of pages a club uploads to the importer, kept so a booking can always be traced back to the paper it came from.
  • Notification subscriptions for coaches who turn alerts on.

What we don't

No payment details, no location, no analytics or advertising trackers, no contact with a club's members or clients, and nothing sold or shared with anyone for any purpose. There is no third-party tracking script on this site or in the app.

Who can see it

A club's data belongs to that club. Front desk staff and directors see their own club; a coach sees their own hours, and the rest of the club's day only if their director has switched that on. None of this is enforced in the browser — every read and every change is checked on the server against who is asking.

Access on our side is limited to what is needed to run and support the service, and we look at a club's actual bookings only when they ask us to help with something specific.

The AI features

Two parts of Courtime send data to OpenAI: reading a photographed page, and the Tempo assistant. What goes out is the photograph or the question, plus the schedule context needed to answer it. OpenAI does not use data sent through their API to train their models. Nothing is sent unless a club uses those features, and a club on the free tier never reaches the assistant at all.

Where it lives, and for how long

Data is held by Convex, our database and backend provider, on infrastructure in the United States. We keep a club's data for as long as they have an account. Delete the club and it goes — bookings, clients, photographs, the lot — within 30 days, including from backups.

Getting it back, or getting rid of it

Ask us and we'll send a club everything we hold for it in a plain file, or delete it. Either way, within 30 days and at no cost. A person named in a club's book who wants their details removed should ask the club, who can delete them directly; if they'd rather come to us, we'll pass it on and follow up.

Children

Junior programmes mean children's names appear in a club's schedule. Those entries are the club's records, entered by the club, and Courtime does not create accounts for anyone under 16 or contact anyone named in a book.

Changes, and getting in touch

If this ever changes in a way that matters, we'll tell every club by email before it takes effect rather than quietly reposting the page. Questions, requests, or anything that looks wrong: alex.rares.giurea@gmail.com.
